Privacy notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to the details you send through the request form on this website. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers; the video and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Muhammad Haider Abbas, founder of Adaptive IDS, based in the United Kingdom. Contact: use the request form on the home page.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Name, work email, organisation, role (optional), organisation typeTo reply to you and understand whether an evaluation fits
What you're interested in and your message (optional)To prepare a useful reply or call
Your consent tick and the time you sent the formTo record that you asked us to contact you

Please don't send network data, credentials or other people's personal data through the form.

Lawful basis

We process these details because you asked us to contact you (your consent, given with the tick box) and because replying to business enquiries is in our legitimate interests. We don't use them for anything else, don't add you to a mailing list, and never sell or share them for marketing.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in a database run by Supabase, our processor, hosted in London (AWS eu-west-2). The database only allows the website to add new requests; it can't read them back. Only the founder can view them. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted. You can ask for deletion at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to or restrict their use, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us as above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

About the product

This notice covers the website only. Adaptive IDS itself runs on your own hardware; during an evaluation no data leaves your organisation, under a separate written authorisation.
